# The prism plan: one sheet of facets that becomes any shape, in a 3D room

The user loves the faceted objects and the way their facets light up (`docs/taste.md`, principle 10). Over 2026-10-09 and 10 they asked for the prism to open into a sheet that wraps round and rejoins, rises into mountains, folds back into any shape, opens up again, and engulfs the camera; and then for the prism to stand in a 3D room with real lights and lasers that reflect off its facets and shine into it. On 2026-10-10, after trying stage 2, they found it "quite busy… always pulsating and moving a lot", "always defaulting back to a kind of flower-looking flat shape with the same pulsing elements from the center", missing the prism's old shapes, and looping the same sets; they asked for it to be able to "just be beautiful and have minimal movement, slight rippling, or facets lighting up symmetrically", to rise to crescendos and come back down, and to improvise shapes rather than loop, and left the decisions below to Claude. This is the plan agreed, in order. Stages 1 and 2 are built (the sheet, then the Prism, `objects/prism.js`); the rest is to come.

## Decided

- **The prism and the Fold become one object, the Prism**, with one shared brain: the music's patterns (the kick's rings and cascades, the stabs' wedges, checker and bands, the hats' sparks, the melody's spiral, life, the improvising symmetric figure, the drop's run-up), the colours, the textures (glass, solid, outline, hologram) and, later, the lighting. (Done in stage 2: one brain in `objects/prism.js`; `fold.js` is gone.)
- **The sheet is the body; the Fold's forms and the prism's gem are shapes it folds into.** The prism's nine shapes (`SHAPES`) are radii by direction, so the sheet's closed form takes them directly, along with the superformula's.
- **Cut the sheet in the gem's own pattern** (the icosahedron's twenty faces, so the closed gem keeps the prism's five-fold look exactly), opening like a flower into a flat five-pointed star of facets. The user's choice to make; Claude recommended this one. Tubes and rings stretch a star more than a square, so for those the swarm moves the facets onto the square sheet (`sheetGeo`).
- **Keep both kinds of light.** The patterns are light *from* the facets (each glows like an LED panel, by rules tied to the music); the room's lamps are light falling *on* them (by where each lamp is and which way each facet faces). A facet's colour is its own glow plus the light it catches. They talk to each other: a spotlight landing sets off a cascade there, a laser leaves a fading trace on the facets it hits, the kick's ring on the prism throws a ring onto the walls, a hologram ignores the lamps. Journey picks the balance per section (calm: soft lamps, faint patterns; intense: the patterns full, the lamps strobing).
- **Few things at once** still holds: a dark room, few lamps, the music bringing things in one at a time.

### Decided on 2026-10-10 (Claude's calls, the user left them open)

- **Why stage 2 felt busy and samey** (the diagnosis): every pattern runs at once and nothing chooses stillness (the kick lifts the surface as well as lighting it, the bass swells it, the hats sparkle every 16th, the melody's spiral always turns, the dance moves it, the form changes every four bars); every section's program has an open form in its second slot and half the breakdowns lay it flat, so the star keeps coming back; most patterns start from the middle, so the open star always reads as a flower pulsing from its centre; a program is a fixed list of three forms cycled, replayed exactly when a section returns; the gem's shapes have only one slot in three.
- **Four systems at their own speeds, and a conductor over them.** The *conductor* (how much may move), the *shape* (what it is), the *facets* (how its light lives), the *space* (where the camera is). Each runs on its own timescale, and the conductor decides how much of each is allowed.
- **Calm is nearly still but alive.** At its calmest the Prism holds its shape and turns slowly, its glints sweeping as it turns (the real sparkle of a cut stone), with one quiet thing moving: a faint ripple, a slow breath, or one symmetric pattern stepping once a bar. The user's words: "minimal movement, slight rippling, or facets lighting up symmetrically".
- **Activity rises and falls in waves, never just climbs.** It rises through a build to a crescendo on the drop, then falls back quickly and settles somewhere new. Planned from the drops read ahead (`audio/foresee.js`), so each climb peaks on its drop and a long calm follows it.
- **Movement has a budget, spent in a ladder.** Each thing that moves costs something, and the budget lets them in one at a time: light before motion, the surface before the shape. The kick lights the facets at any level, but moves the surface only once things have built. 16ths sparkle, the beat lights, the bar steps a pattern, the phrase mutates the shape, the section brings a new theme, the track is the arc of climbs and releases.
- **Symmetric at rest; symmetry may break at a crescendo and resolve after.** Principle 9 holds as the base; breaking it is the tension, finding it again the release.
- **The flat star is a moment, not home.** It's one place the improviser can reach (breakdowns favour it), weighted low; the gem's shapes and the in-betweens are where it mostly lives.
- **Shape as genes, improvised by mutation.** A shape is a short recipe of genes (how closed, rolled, bent, twisted; the superformula's points and sharpness; its symmetry; its relief: mountains, ridges, rings, spikes pushed out; squash and stretch). Diamonds, cubes, stars, discs, flowers, urchins, tubes and rings are points in that space, and so are the shapes between them nobody designed. Improvising is a walk from where it is: small steps most of the time, bigger leaps on phrase lines and bigger still at a crescendo. It remembers where it has been and prefers to move away (so it doesn't loop). A section has a theme (its home shape) and comes back to it with variations, not an exact repeat. The shape genes are also the short recipe an AI could write (Jev, below).
- **Facets are cells with rules.** Each facet has a state (how lit, its phase, its colour) and is moved by its neighbours; the music feeds energy in at symmetric points, never only the middle. A section's rule gives its character: slowly growing spots and stripes (reaction-diffusion: beautiful and nearly still, for calm), ripples spreading and fading, cascades (as now), frost growing along edges, and fireflies that fall into step (each facet blinking on its own clock; through a build they synchronise until the whole Prism flashes as one on the drop, then scatter: the crescendo made visible).
- **The space follows the arc** (stage 7, the old stage 3): far off a jewel; drawn in through a build until facets fill the screen; through a facet that swings open on the drop and inside it, faceted walls round the camera; unrolled into flat land in a breakdown, ranges of facets rising out of the ground like mountains or city blocks; rolled back up round the camera and pulled out to see it close into a new shape.
- **Textures and light per facet.** Textures sweep across facet by facet as new detail does: glass, solid, outline and hologram as now, then frosted ice, mirror (the world's colours in it), stained glass (lead lines, saturated panes), iridescent (colour by angle), LED panels, brushed metal. Light comes three ways: the facet's own glow (the cells), light falling on it (the lamps), and glints as it turns; later its fire (each facet splitting light into a rainbow).

## Stages

1. **The sheet** (built: the Fold). Eight forms eased into one another: sheet, peaks, waves, tube, ring, halo, twist, ball.
2. **Merge into one Prism** on the gem-cut sheet (built, 2026-10-10): the gem closed, opening like a flower into the star, the sheet's forms, folding back into any closed shape; one shared brain. The cut is five gores from the front corner to the back (`gemSheetGeo`), so the open star is a five-petal pinwheel (each gore turns a tenth of a turn along its length, as the icosahedron's faces zigzag). The tube, ring, halo and twist stay on the square sheet; changing body is a quick shatter and gather, until the swarm (stage 11) can fly the facets across.
3. **The conductor: calm by default** (built, 2026-10-10: `TUNE.prism.act`, see CLAUDE.md's Facets). One activity level for the Prism (from the tension, the section's character and the pace, eased), and a budget the patterns and movements draw on in a ladder: at rest it holds its shape, turns slowly and glints, with one quiet pattern; the kick lights it without moving it; ripples, sparkle, the dance, form changes and shattering come in only as the activity rises. Fewer open forms (the star a moment, not a slot in every section), patterns seeded at symmetric points rather than only the middle. The quick fix for what the user saw; mostly tuning and gating in `objects/prism.js`.
4. **Shape genes and the improviser** (built, 2026-10-10: `improvise()`, `TUNE.prism.imp`; see CLAUDE.md's Facets. With it, stage 3's calm was redone: the user found it still busy all the time, because steady full-on techno's tension sits high, so the activity now follows the music moving, not the tension's level). Replace the fixed three-form programs with shape genes and a walk through them: small steps, leaps on phrase lines and at crescendos, a memory of where it's been, a section's theme and its variations. Bring back all of the prism's shapes, and the shapes between them.
5. **Facets as cells.** Per-facet state and neighbour rules on the automata's level: slow spots and stripes for calm, ripples, cascades, frost, and the synchronising fireflies for crescendos; a section's rule chosen to suit it, the music feeding energy in at symmetric points.
6. **The arc.** The conductor planned from the drops read ahead: each build climbing to peak on its drop (more facets, the fireflies falling into step, the shape's leaps growing), the release after, a long calm, symmetry breaking at the peak and resolving after. Live tension where nothing was read ahead.
7. **The room, the camera and the lamps.** A void with haze, a box, a prism inside a prism, or the sheet closing round the camera. A 3D camera that flies round, in close, through a facet that swings open, and inside; following the arc (far off, drawn in, inside on the drop, the flat land in a breakdown, rolled back up). Lamps that light the facets (glints as a spot sweeps, coloured sides), mirror-ball spots thrown onto the walls by the facets, shadows on the floor, beams in the haze. Needs proper depth (the facet engine sorts far to near today).
8. **Textures:** frosted ice, mirror, stained glass, iridescent, LED panels, brushed metal, each sweeping across facet by facet; the fire (dispersion) per facet.
9. **3D lasers.** Rays traced each frame against the facets: bouncing off at the true angle, entering the glass and bending, splitting into a rainbow (a real prism), trapped inside and drawing a symmetric web, flaring where they touch. The 2D lasers' shows (fan, crossing, star, scan) aimed at the prism; a drop fires them all through it.
10. **Journey's light show.** The lamps and lasers on the music: the kick strobes, stabs swing the spots, the hats sparkle the facets, a build turns every lamp onto it, a breakdown leaves one soft spot. Then **other layers in 3D:** orbits and comets as lights circling it (lighting it as they pass), fireflies as motes in the haze, the stargate's rings rushing past the camera, rain through the light.
11. **The swarm and wrapping onto objects.** Facets let go and fly as shards into any shape, including ones a single skin can't make (separate pieces, many limbs); the swarm also carries the facets between the gem and the square instead of the shatter; the closed sheet wraps onto the skull, the heart, the manta or a Blender model.
