Mix Lab · Undertow

A round 4 song mixed the traditional way. Every sound is native: the analog drum machine's kick, the cymbals, the forest percussion, the FM bass, the bowed strings, bamboo and marimba. Every processor is native too. Each part has its own channel: input gain, a low cut, EQ and a compressor. The kick keys a sidechain compressor on the bass, the chords, the leads and the room, so the mix pumps and clears. The drums and the music each go into a bus with its own compressor, the echo and room are sends, and everything ends on the master bus.
