Studio Live

Every sound here is computed from its physics as you play: struck skins, bars, bells, plucked strings, FM and additive tones. Each track loops at its own length, so a 6-step tom against a 12-step bell makes a polyrhythm. Tap a step to add a note, again for an accent, again to clear. Open Sound to shape a track while it plays.

Keep for Claude

Keep a pattern you like, with a word on what you're after. Claude reads it and builds a whole track around it, rendered at full quality.
