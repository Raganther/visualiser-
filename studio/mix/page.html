Mix Lab · Undertow

The round 4 songs mixed the traditional way: Clearing, Two rivers and Undertow, each with its own mix. Play each on its own sounds (the cymbals, the forest percussion, the FM bass, the bowed strings, bamboo and marimba) or on the analog set (the analog drum machine's hats and percussion, the analog synth's bass, pad and leads); the kick is the analog drum machine's in both. Every processor is native too. Each part has its own channel: input gain, a low cut, EQ and a compressor. The kick keys a sidechain compressor on the bass, the chords, the leads and the room, so the mix pumps and clears. The drums and the music each go into a bus with its own compressor, the echo and room are sends, and everything ends on the master bus.
